01 / HOW I THINK

Good work is
clear on purpose.

A few principles I try to bring to every project.

01 / CLARITY
↗

Make the complex understandable

Start with the actual problem. Explain choices in plain language and make the next step obvious.

02 / CRAFT
✳

Care about the details

Hierarchy, spacing, accessibility, loading speed, and small interactions are part of the experience—not decoration.

03 / HONESTY
◎

Show the work, not the hype

Separate experiments from finished work, document limitations, and never invent metrics or outcomes.
