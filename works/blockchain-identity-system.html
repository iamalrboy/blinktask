CASE STUDY / 01 · ACADEMIC EXPLORATION

Blockchain-based
identity verification.

A concept exploration of how a verifiable identity workflow might represent claims, check integrity, and make verification easier to reason about.

i

Project status: This page describes a concept direction, not a security-audited or production identity service. Implementation details, personal contribution, and test evidence must be added after verification.

The problem space

Identity workflows need to balance integrity, verification, privacy, and usability. A ledger may help make some changes auditable, but it does not automatically make submitted identity data true or remove privacy risks.

Concept direction

  • Represent an identity claim separately from the act of verifying it.
  • Explore integrity checks and traceable verification events.
  • Keep sensitive personal information off a public ledger wherever possible.
  • Make the verification outcome and its limitations understandable to a user.

What needs evidence

  • Architecture, libraries, and data model actually implemented.
  • Which parts were built by me versus teammates or tutorials.
  • Tests, threat model, privacy review, and demonstrated results.

No claims of production readiness, regulatory compliance, or security assurance are made.
