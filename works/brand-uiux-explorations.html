Design approach

  • Start with audience, context, and the problem to solve.
  • Build a small set of consistent visual rules before producing variations.
  • Consider hierarchy, readability, responsive behavior, and accessibility.
  • Explain the trade-offs behind a design, not only the final appearance.

What each published project should include

  • Brief and constraints, including whether it was self-initiated or commissioned.
  • Exploration, iterations, and the rationale for decisions.
  • Final assets or working screens, with permission to share.
  • Specific contribution and any outcome supported by evidence.
